# HevenOps — Product & Feature Overview Report

**A complete, plain-language guide to everything HevenOps can do**
*(Prepared for non-technical readers — no technical terms inside)*

---

## 1. What is HevenOps?

**HevenOps is an all-in-one management app for Paying Guest (PG) accommodations, hostels, and shared rental properties.**

Running a PG today usually means juggling notebooks, Excel sheets, phone calls, and endless WhatsApp chats. HevenOps replaces all of that with **one simple app** that lets a property owner:

- Set up their building (floors → rooms → beds) digitally
- Admit tenants and track who lives where
- Collect rent automatically and send WhatsApp payment reminders
- Track security deposits, expenses, electricity bills, and staff salaries
- Handle complaints and repairs through a simple ticket system
- See profit/loss, occupancy trends, and alerts on one dashboard
- Store important documents (ID proofs, agreements, receipts) safely in one place
- Give limited access to managers, accountants, and staff

> **In one sentence:** HevenOps is a phone-first operating system for PG owners — it replaces notebooks, spreadsheets, and WhatsApp chaos with structured tenant records, automated rent tracking, disciplined money books, a repair desk, vacancy planning, and role-based dashboards.

## 2. Who Uses It? (User Roles)

The app shows different things to different people, based on their role:

| Role | Who They Are | What They Can See & Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | The property boss | Everything — all screens, all money details, team management, reports |
| **Manager** | Day-to-day caretaker | Tenants, operations, occupancy; sees money numbers as summary cards but **no profit/cash charts or Finance tab** |
| **Accountant** | The money person | Full Finance section — rent, expenses, salaries, deposits, reports; but no tenant/operations management |
| **Maintenance Staff** | Repair workers | Sees **only repair jobs assigned to them**; nothing else |
| **Housekeeping Staff** | Cleaning workers | Same restricted view as maintenance — only their assigned jobs |

**Key point:** Tenants do not use the app themselves. They only *receive* automatic WhatsApp messages (rent reminders, payment confirmations, etc.).

## 4. Detailed Feature Descriptions & Use Cases

---

### Feature 1: Sign-Up & Login (Password-Free)

**What it does:**
- Users log in using just their **mobile number**.
- A **6-digit secret code arrives over WhatsApp**, which they enter to log in.
- First-time users simply add their **name and email** — done.

**Why it's useful:**
Non-technical PG owners never need to remember passwords. Logging in feels as easy as receiving a normal message.

**Built-in protections:**
- Code expires after a few minutes.
- Wrong attempts are limited (prevents misuse).
- Re-sending the code has a short wait time.

---

### Feature 2: Property Setup (Digital Building Builder)

**What it does:**
- **Add a property:** name, address, city, landmark, contact number.
- **Add floors:** any floor number, including basements.
- **Add rooms:** for each room you set:
  - Sharing capacity (Single / Double / Triple / up to N beds)
  - AC or Non-AC
  - Furnished or not
  - Attached bathroom or not
  - Monthly rent per bed
- **Beds are created automatically** when a room is added — matching its capacity.
- Rooms can be edited later (change price, amenities, capacity).

**Why it's useful:**
It mirrors your real building inside the app. Everything else — occupancy, rent, vacancy — works off this true picture. No more guessing how many beds are free.

**Safety built-in:**
Beds can't be removed if any tenant ever stayed there — history is always protected.

---

### Feature 3: Team Management & Controlled Access (Owner Only)

**What it does:**
- Owner **invites teammates by phone number**, choosing their role (Manager / Accountant / Maintenance / Housekeeping).
- The invited person accepts via a secure link (valid for **72 hours**).
- Owner can change anyone's role anytime.
- Owner can **suspend or remove** a member without deleting their work history.

**Why it's useful:**
Controlled delegation. The accountant gets money screens, the manager runs daily ops, the cleaner sees only their jobs — nobody sees more than they should, and nobody can touch another owner's property.

---

### Feature 4: Tenant Records (Complete Guest Directory)

**What it does:**
- Add a tenant with name + phone (required), plus optional email, permanent address, and emergency contact.
- Each tenant has a status: **Active** (living there), **Former** (moved out), or **Archived** (removed).
- Staff can mark a tenant as verified/unverified.
- Smart search: find any tenant instantly by name or phone.
- **Smart duplicate prevention:** if a returning guest's phone already exists, the system reuses their profile instead of creating a duplicate.

**Why it's useful:**
One clean, searchable directory of everyone who ever stayed — instead of scattered notebooks and chat histories.

**Deleting safely:**
"Archiving" a tenant frees their bed automatically but keeps their full history intact.

---

### Feature 5: Bed Allocation (Move-In Made Simple)

**What it does:**
- Pick any vacant bed and move a tenant in with:
  - Joining date
  - Agreed monthly rent
- The bed picker only shows **empty beds** — double booking is impossible.
- Each tenant can hold only one bed at a time (must check out first).
- Moving a tenant in **automatically opens their security-deposit account**.
- If two staff members try to grab the same bed at once, the second person is warned and asked to re-pick.

**Why it's useful:**
Fast, mistake-proof check-ins. The app also shows a building-style view (floor → room → bed) so you literally "walk the building" on screen and see exactly who sits where.

---

### Feature 6: Rent Collection (Automatic Billing & Payment Tracking)

**What it does:**
- **One tap generates the month's rent dues** for every active tenant (due date defaults to the 5th). This can also happen **fully automatically** every month.
- Every charge moves through clear statuses: **Due → Partially Paid → Paid** (or **Overdue** past the due date).
- Record payments with amount, date, and method (**Cash / UPI / Bank Transfer / Card / Other**) plus reference numbers and notes.
- **Partial payments supported** — pay ₹3,000 now, rest later.
- **Special "Settle month" option:** if a tenant staying only part of the month pays a reduced full amount (e.g., ₹5,000 of ₹8,000), mark the month as settled with one switch — documented, not messy.
- **Adjust the billed rent** with a reason (e.g., tenant stayed only 18 days) — cannot go below what was already collected.
- **Payment reversals:** wrong entry? Reverse it with a reason. The original stays visible, the correction is recorded, nothing is silently erased.
- Duplicate submissions are automatically prevented — a shaky internet connection can never double-charge a tenant.
- **Monthly rent summary:** expected vs collected vs pending vs overdue, with counts of each type of tenant.

**Why it's useful:**
This replaces the rent notebook entirely. Owners see collection health instantly and know exactly whom to chase.

**Door-to-door mode:**
Rent can be viewed floor-by-floor, room-by-room — perfect for physically going door-to-door collecting rent with the right list in hand.

---

### Feature 7: Security Deposits (Dispute-Free Settlements)

**What it does:**
Every tenant stay has its own deposit ledger showing:
- Total collected
- Deductions made (for damages/unpaid dues)
- Refunded so far
- **Current refundable balance**

Staff record four kinds of entries, each with amount, date, and reason:
1. Collection
2. Additional collection
3. Deduction
4. Refund

When nothing remains refundable, the account marks itself **settled**. Any mistaken entry can be formally reversed (with a required reason).

**Why it's useful:**
At move-out there are zero arguments — both sides see exactly what came in, what was deducted, and what must be refunded.

---

### Feature 8: Move-Out Handling (Notice to Exit, Cleanly)

**What it does:**
- Record a **notice date** and **planned exit date** — the tenant appears everywhere as "leaving soon" while still occupying the bed.
- Before finalizing checkout, staff see a **settlement preview**: total unpaid rent + refundable deposit = exact amount to hand back.
- Confirm actual exit date (+ optional notes). The bed becomes available instantly, and the tenant becomes "former."
- Full stay history for every tenant is kept forever.

**Why it's useful:**
Clean exits with money matters visible upfront — no surprises for owner or tenant.

---

### Feature 9: Operating Expenses (Know Where Money Goes)

**What it does:**
- Record bills by category: Cleaning, Repairs, Electricity, Water, Wi-Fi, Gas, Groceries, Staff, Maintenance, Tax, Other.
- Capture vendor name, description, bill date, due date, and amount.
- **Pay in parts or full**, with method and reference tracking.
- Statuses: Due → Partial → Paid (or Overdue).
- Wrong payments can be reversed with a reason.

**Why it's useful:**
Track running costs and outstanding supplier bills — never lose a receipt again.

---

### Feature 10: Utility Bills (Never Miss Electricity Day)

**What it does:**
Dedicated tracking for recurring utilities:
- Types: Electricity, Water, Wi-Fi, Gas, Maintenance, Other
- Vendor, billing period (month), bill date, due date, amount
- Partial payments, status tracking, payment history, reversals
- Unpaid utility totals appear as **dashboard alerts**

**Why it's useful:**
Electricity and water bills arrive monthly and pile up silently. This makes them impossible to forget.

---

### Feature 11: Salaries & Advances (Payroll Without Excel)

**What it does:**
- **Employee register:** name, phone, job title (cook/cleaner/guard/etc.), monthly salary, active flag.
- **Generate monthly salary dues** for all staff in one tap (or automatically each month).
- Record payouts partially or fully (cash/UPI/bank/other).
- **Salary advances:** lend money to an employee with amount/date/note; track recovery until closed.
- Monthly payroll summary: total due, paid, pending, advances given.
- Employees can be deactivated/reactivated rather than deleted — history preserved.

**Why it's useful:**
Handles the very common "advance please" request professionally, and turns payday into a few taps instead of a spreadsheet session.

---

### Feature 12: Maintenance Tickets (Complaint-to-Fix Tracking)

**What it does:**
- **Raise a ticket:** title, description, category, priority (**Low / Medium / High / Urgent**), optional link to a room/bed/tenant, estimated cost, deadline.
- **Assign** it to any active employee.
- Work flows through clear stages: Open → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved → Closed (or Cancelled).
- Every ticket keeps a **timestamped timeline**: creation, edits, assignments, comments, resolution — a complete work journal.
- **Resolve** with a write-up + actual cost — and optionally **auto-create the matching expense entry** so repair costs hit the books automatically.
- Attach photos/invoices as ticket documents.
- Closed tickets become read-only.
- **Maintenance/Housekeeping logins see only their own assigned tickets** — a simple personal task list.

**Why it's useful:**
Track "AC not cooling" from complaint → assignment → fix → cost booked, all in one thread. Tenants stop repeating complaints; nothing falls through cracks.

### Feature 13: Private Document Vault (Paperwork, Safe & Sorted)

**What it does:**
- Upload documents (PDF, JPG, PNG — up to 10 MB) attached to:
  - A tenant (ID proofs, agreements)
  - An employee (contracts)
  - A repair ticket (photos, invoices)
  - Or just the property generally
- Documents are stored **privately** — only your team can open them.
- Download links expire after **5 minutes** for safety.
- Delete obsolete documents anytime (owner/accountant only, always recorded).

**Why it's useful:**
KYC papers, agreements, and receipts live in one safe place — instead of buried in a WhatsApp gallery.

### Feature 14: WhatsApp Messaging & Automatic Reminders

**What it does:**

*Automatic rent reminders* (per-property settings):
- Master ON/OFF switch
- Remind **N days before** due date (multiple reminders possible)
- Remind **on** the due date
- Remind again **N days after** becoming overdue
- Default schedule: 3 days before, on due date, then 3 and 7 days late
- Tenants who opted out never receive messages

*Manual messaging:*
Five ready-made templates:
1. Rent Reminder
2. Payment Confirmation
3. Utility Notice
4. Maintenance Update
5. Move-Out Reminder

Send instantly to any phone, or **schedule for later**.

*Reliability built-in:*
- Messages retry automatically several times if sending fails
- Delivery receipts tracked: Sent → Delivered → Read / Failed
- Complete message log per property, filterable by status
- Duplicate messages prevented automatically

**Why it's useful:**
Chasing rent happens automatically instead of through awkward phone calls. And there's written proof of exactly what was told to whom, and when.

## 5. Trust & Safety Features (Built Into Everything)

These behaviors run quietly in the background and protect the owner's books:

| Protection | What It Means in Plain Language |
|---|---|
| **Complete audit trail** | Every change — who made it, what changed, before and after — is permanently recorded |
| **Immutable money history** | Financial records are never edited or deleted; mistakes are fixed with official, linked reversals |
| **Duplicate protection** | Retried actions (bad network, double tap) can never create duplicate charges, payments, or messages |
| **Deposit math guardrails** | Deductions can never exceed what was actually collected |
| **Strict privacy isolation** | Nobody can ever see another owner's property data |
| **Exact money precision** | Amounts support exact paise values — accounting-grade accuracy |

## 6. Key Day-to-Day Workflows (How a User Experiences It)

### Workflow 1: Getting Started
Sign up with phone + WhatsApp code → add property → add floors and rooms (beds appear automatically) → invite manager/accountant/housekeeper → they join within 72 hours.

### Workflow 2: Moving a Guest In
Create tenant profile → pick a vacant bed → set joining date & agreed rent → collect and record the security deposit. Done in minutes.

### Workflow 3: Monthly Rent Rhythm
Rent dues generate automatically at month start → WhatsApp reminders go out on schedule → record UPI/cash payments as they arrive (partial OK) → chase whoever appears on the dashboard's overdue list.

### Workflow 4: When a Guest Leaves
Record notice → tenant shows on move-out list → at exit, open settlement preview (unpaid rent + refundable deposit) → check out → deduct damages if any → refund balance → bed becomes sellable again.

### Workflow 5: Running the Back Office
Log vendor and utility bills as they come, pay gradually → generate payroll monthly, pay salaries, handle advances → raise repair tickets, assign staff, resolve with costs auto-booked.

### Workflow 6: Reviewing the Business
Check dashboard alerts daily → study occupancy and profit trends → export spreadsheet reports for the accountant or tax filing.
